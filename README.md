Frequently asked questions
What is Bubibu?
Bubibu is a coloring and creativity game for children aged 3 to 8. Kids help Bubi, a little paint drop, bring eight gloomy worlds back to color across 240 levels and 13 kinds of games: coloring, letter and number tracing, puzzles, memory cards, music and more.
What is free and what is in Bubibu Club?

The first 8 levels of the first world and the first level of every other world are free. Bubibu Club is an optional monthly or yearly subscription that unlocks all 8 worlds, all 240 levels and every game.

How do I subscribe?
On the home screen, tap the yellow Club button, then Grown-ups. After the grown-up check, choose Monthly or Yearly. Payment is handled by Apple.

What is the grown-up check?
Purchases, settings and links are protected so children can't open them by accident. The check shows three numbers written as words (for example "seven, two, nine"). Tap the matching digits to continue.

I already subscribed. How do I restore my purchase?
Open the Grown-ups area, pass the grown-up check and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and select Bubibu Club. You can cancel at any time; you keep access until the end of the current period. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can we play on more than one device?
Yes. Bubibu Club works on every iPhone and iPad signed in with the same Apple ID; use Restore Purchases on the new device. Game progress and paintings are saved on each device separately.

Will my child's progress be lost?
Progress and paintings are stored on the device and stay there between updates. Deleting the app removes them.

How do I turn off the music or change the language?
Open the Grown-ups area. There you can switch the music on or off and choose English, Turkish, Spanish or Portuguese.

Does Bubibu have ads or collect my child's data?
No. Bubibu has no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Read our Privacy Policy.
